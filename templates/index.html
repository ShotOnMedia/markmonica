How it works

Set it up once. Let the memories come to you.

Memories' Events keeps the guest experience deliberately simple, while giving the host one private place to manage everything.

01

Create your event

Add your event name, date, cover photo, colours and a heading style that fits the occasion.

02

Share one QR code

Put it on invitations, tables or signage. Guests scan it with their phone camera — no app or account required.

03

Keep every memory

Photos and videos arrive in your private dashboard where you can browse, search, delete or download them together.
